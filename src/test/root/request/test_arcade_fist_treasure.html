Dungeon Fist!

You head south for a bit, and come to an archway that would presumably lead into a room, except the opening is blocked by a seamless sheet of some strange light-blue metal. It glows faintly in the dim light -- very strange.

You gradually become aware of a strange buzzing sound just on the edge of your hearing, and a faint vibration in the pocket of your... do loincloths have pockets?

Well, whatever. You take out the key you found before, which is humming with faint magical power. As you reach out and touch it to the wall of blue metal, there's a bright flash of light and a sound best-described as "FWANG."

When your vision clears, the wall is gone. You step through the archway into a small room, which contains a large wooden treasure chest overflowing with gold and colorful jewels! Sweet! You stuff as much as you can into the pockets of your... well, the pockets of your loincloth, I guess.

That accomplished, you prepare to return back up the hall to the north.

Incidentally: It occurs to you that you're hungrier than you've ever been before. It really sucks!
